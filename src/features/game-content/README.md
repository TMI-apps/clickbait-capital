# Game content

The full Dutch deck and all player-facing copy for Clickbait Capital, as typed data. Rules SSOT: `documentation/DOC_GAME_DESIGN.md`; the engine and its content schema live in `src/features/game-engine/`.

## Purpose

- Supplies `GAME_CONTENT`, a `GameContent` the engine plays: 95 cards (viral 32 and news 32: 8 posts for phases 1–3, 4 posts per phase and 8 decision cards; ads 31: 7 posts for phases 1–3, 4 per phase and 8 decisions; per type the pool is 12 / 14 / 18 / 8 cards in phases 1–4 (ads one less in 1–3), with 0 / 2 / 6 / 4 decisions; the hand's type mix is one roll, mostly one of each, and each type keeps its own draw pile where shown cards go to the bottom, deck-draw D1–D4), 28 events, the four phases and the balance constants.
- Adds presentation the engine does not need: flavor text and image hints (`imageTheme`, `imageAlt`) per card and event id, the official announcement (`announce`) and the real event with its article (`realCase`) of a decision card, phase intros, the three game-over texts, the IPO end report, all UI copy (`UI_COPY`) and the glossary of hard words (`GLOSSARY`).
- Audience: pupils of about 11 (groep 7/8). Short sentences, everyday Dutch, situations from their world; hard words are marked and explained on tap (kids-content D1–D3).
- No UI, no React. Board and screen laps import `@/features/game-content/api`.

### Events

Milestones open soon after their phase starts. Every other event is a **scandal** (score-redesign D14): while hidden reputation is below `CONTENT_BALANCE.scandal.threshold` (30), each card turn rolls against the scandal chance (0 → 90 % over 20 points) and draws a scandal of the phase, at most one event per card turn and spaced by `eventPacing` (game-engine D10): 3–6 card turns after the previous event, only 3 at the maximum chance. Each scandal also costs a fine of 18 revenue points at the phase's euro rate (D25). Scandals repeat once all of a phase's scandals have fired.

## Decisions

- **Decision cards (policy-cards D1–D4, § 3–§ 9; decision-cards lap):** from phase 2 each type's pool also holds company decisions (24 cards, ids `<type>-besluit-*`): 2 / 6 / 4 per type in phases 2–4, so decisions are 0 / 14 / 33 / 50 % of the pool; the 8 (ads 7) "every phase" posts now run phases 1–3, texts unchanged. A decision card is a normal card of its type (same profile, effects, modifiers); only `announce` marks it: the feed posts the company's spin as an official post, the card says what it really does. No engine flag, no "beleid" label (D1). `realCase` gives the card detail an "Echt gebeurd" line and one article link (D3); the line is plain text without brand names (asserted), the article may name companies, so its url is left out of the trademark scan. Sources were picked open and reputable and checked by hand once (`documentation/jobs/temp_job_decision-cards/BRIEF.md` § Source check): paywalled TIME and MIT Technology Review and the company newsroom were swapped for NPR, CBS News and TechCrunch; the Myanmar case links a gentler NPR explainer, so its line now cites UN investigators. 12 new scandals (phases 2–4) cover the harm; heavy topics are fictional and non-graphic (D4: "meiden worden lastiggevallen door onbekende mannen", "geweld in een ver land"). New glossary words: _privé_, _moderator_, _lobby_. Event bodies E2, E4, E9, E10 and E12 name the player's app with `{app}` (app-name lap). Decision values sit at the level of the phase posts next to them (balance § Decision-cards lap), not under them.
- **App name copy (app-name lap):** `UI_COPY.appName` holds the naming step (title, label, hint, dice and Start buttons, the five refusal reasons). `DEFAULT_APP_NAME` ("Jouw app") is the name of a run without one. `{app}` appears in the four phase intros, two game-over headlines, the end report intro, the resume text, the share text, the feed label and two follower lines. `selectGameOverText` and `buildEndReport` fill it from `EndReportInput.appName`. The brand list moved from `gameContent.test.ts` to `services/brandNames.ts` (`REAL_BRAND_NAMES`), so the copy test and the app-name filter share it. The privacy page names the app name among what the browser keeps.
- **Exponential growth (exponential-growth D1–D4, supersedes the numbers of D5, D8, D9 and score-redesign D20):** a run starts at 0 users and € 3.000 (about 15 turns of phase-1 server costs at € 200, so a new app has runway but bankruptcy stays real). A users point adds 6 % of the current users plus a fixed 50 / 500 / 5.000 / 50.000 per phase; news cards now grow users a little (2–5 points, always below every viral card) instead of costing users; ads pay € 0,05 per user per point and cost 1,5 % of the users per point. Goals are ×10 per phase (10k / € 20k → 100k / € 200k → 1 mln / € 2 mln → 10 mln / € 20 mln); costs € 200 / 4.000 / 40.000 / 400.000 per turn; scandals from reputation 35, fines 8 points of the phase price plus 8 ad points from the current users. Simulation (60 seeds): human-like 57 × won in a median 38 turns (8–11 per phase), rule-of-thumb 33 × won, mixed 55, rotate 46; viral-only, ad-only and news-heavy 60 × Bankroet (viral-only meets a scandal in 59 of 60 runs). Precedent: idle/incremental games (Cookie Clicker, AdVenture Capitalist) grow by a share of what you own and set each milestone an order of magnitude higher, so every step takes similar time. Copy follows the numbers: the phase 1 intro starts at 0 users, phase 3 no longer claims millions (players arrive with 100k–500k).
- **Trillen copy (juice-wiring-flow D3):** `UI_COPY.settings.vibrationHint` explains the new Trillen row; the reset hint and the privacy page now name vibration among the settings kept on the device.
- **Typed TS data, not JSON (game-content D1):** the structure whitelist has no data folder, and TS checks the deck against the engine schema at compile time, like `seedContent.ts`.
- **Presentation beside the schema (D2):** flavor and image hints are extra fields on the same objects (`ContentCard = CardDefinition & CardPresentation`); the engine ignores them, so its schema did not change. The imagery lap keys images by card and event id (`@/features/card-imagery/api` `getCardImage`); its alt text describes the real photo, so `imageAlt` stays a sourcing hint.
- **Intended losses (D3, score-redesign D16):** Bankroet is the only game over, so every one-sided strategy goes bankrupt for its own reason: viral-only earns nothing and its scandals cost fines, ad-only drives the audience away so ads stop paying (D20), news-heavy never earns. `balanceSimulation.test.ts` asserts all three. Was: viral-only → Verbod, ad-only → Verlaten.
- **"Not modelled yet" as events (D4):** competitors and ad-pressure complaints (phase 2), fines and hearings (phase 3) are Toxicity-triggered events. The engine cannot trigger on falling engagement, so the competitor event fires early in phase 2 for any player who ignored news (Toxicity > 15).
- **Content owns its balance (D5):** `CONTENT_BALANCE` and `CONTENT_PHASES` start from the engine's `SEED_CONTENT`; changed: server costs phase 3 −6 and phase 4 −7 revenue per turn (seed −5 / −6), and phase 1 `usersPerPoint` 2,000 (seed 5,000). With the seed costs a careful player won every simulated run in the minimum number of turns; with these, a naive rotation reaches the IPO phase but goes bankrupt there, and only reactive play wins. With 5,000 users per point phase 1 lasted two turns, so its nine cards per type and the first event never showed; 2,000 gives 4 turns for a pure viral rush and about 11 for balanced play. Superseded in part by D8.
- **IPO hold 8 turns (shuffled-hand D4; superseded by score-redesign D17: goal lines, no holds):** a random hand does not always hold the card a meter needs, so holding Reputatie > 60 and Inkomsten > 80 took longer (human-like 34 → 49 turns with a 10-turn hold); 8 brings it to 44. Rule-of-thumb still wins 54 / 60 but its IPO phase is long (43 turns median): it has no lookahead to pick between two cards of the same type.
- **Run length from play-tests (D8, balance-pass lap):** target a 25–45-turn run for a decent player who sees only the board. Phase 3 hold 30 → 12 turns (orchestrator decision for run length; design § 6 updated); phase 1 `usersPerPoint` 2,000 → 2,500 (balanced V/A/N leaves phase 1 in 19 instead of 25 turns; a viral rush still takes 4); phase 4 server cost −7 → −6 and reputation loss ×3 → ×2.5 (a shorter phase 3 leaves less buffer before the IPO; with −7 and ×3 a rule-of-thumb player stalled there; with −5 even the naive V/A/N rotation wins every game). The `humanLike` and `ruleOfThumb` strategies are the yardsticks (rule-of-thumb: 9 turns in phase 1, ~55 per run). `humanLike` is the same one-step lookahead as `mixed`, scored only on meters, stats, phase and goal streak. Instead of reading Toxicity, it plays news once four turns have passed without it. Known and not fixed in content: a 1-in-5 viral rotation (V/A/N/N/A) never leaves phase 1, because news and ads must cost users (card profile) and Inkomsten/Reputatie gains above 100 are lost; the fix is feedback on the board (show a capped gain), not more growth on news or ads. Its `usersPerPoint` values are superseded by D9.
- **Phase markets (D9, users-stat-model lap; superseded by score-redesign D11, the users stat no longer exists):** the users stat is read off the Gebruikers meter (engine D1), so each phase sets `usersAtFullMeter`, the market behind a full meter: 115k / 5.3M / 22.5M / 30M. That puts the users goals at Gebruikers 87 (100k), 95 (5M) and 89 (20M). Players enter phases 2–4 at Gebruikers 85–95 (simulation p10–p90), so a goal below ~90 would already hold on entry and phase 2 would hinge on the € 500k alone; 95 makes the phase 2 users goal real (met on entry in 13 of 60 human-like games, 3 of 60 rule-of-thumb). Phase 3 always starts with its goal holding, because phase 2 ends at Gebruikers ≥ 95, which is above 89; its challenge is keeping Gebruikers ≥ 89 for 12 turns while ads and news cost users. A 22M market (goal at 91) doubled the rule-of-thumb run in phase 3 (40 turns); 23M (goal at 87) made the hold free. `gameContent.test.ts` asserts every users goal stays below its market; the simulation reports `usersGoalMetOnEntry` per phase. Revenue values unchanged (engine D1: revenue stays accumulated). `UI_COPY.results.usersLabel` is "Hoogste bereik": the results screen and end report show the run's peak reach (game-board D11). Bereik starts at 57.500 (Gebruikers 50 in the 115k market) instead of 1.000; accepted, since the 100k goal keeps the "nobody knows you yet" scale.
- **UI copy as one constant (D6):** `UI_COPY` grouped by screen, placeholders `{name}` filled with `fillTemplate`. No i18n library: the vision fixes Dutch only. The tutorial line describes drag to play and tap for details with "In de feed" (game-board D2, replaced by thumbnail-imagery D9). `results.reportButton` was added for the ending → results step of the game-flow screens. The share-card lap added the share status lines (`shareDoneStatus`, `shareSavedAndCopiedStatus`, `shareSavedStatus`, `shareFailedStatus`; `shareCopied` now ends in a full stop), `shareCardFooter` and `shareFileName`. `UI_COPY.credits` also holds the `/credits` page headings (`imagesHeading`, `imagesIntro`, `audioHeading`); the image credits themselves come from `@/features/card-imagery/api`; `imagesIntro` names the allowed licences (CC0, CC BY, Unsplash: thumbnail-imagery D10). The game-settings lap added `howToPlay`, `appStatus`, the motion / colour-mode / wipe keys under `settings` and the start-screen links (`start.howToPlayButton` now reads "Hoe speel je?"). The local-save lap added `resume` (the `/spel` resume offer) and `board.resumedAnnouncement`, uses `start.continueButton` / `start.newGameButton`, removed the untrue `board.quitConfirm` and names the saved game in `privacy`. The event-redesign lap replaced `events.optionKinds` (the option kind labels gave the effect away) with `events.outcomeKicker` ("Je koos") and `events.continueButton` ("Verder") for the outcome step (game-flow, event-redesign D1–D2). Every `privacy` claim must match the code; the audit lives in `documentation/DOC_DECISION_LOG.md` § launch-basics. The score-redesign end lap (D33) added the how-to-play sections `score` (the chart, hold-to-forecast, Bankroet) and `scandal`, a forecast line in `tutorial`, the peak users in `results.shareText` (`{users}`) and the money in the end report intro (`{money}`); it deleted the unused `meters.reputation`, the meter descriptions, `board.usersStatLabel` / `revenueStatLabel`, `board.tapHint` and `board.effectLabels`. Phase tips no longer name reputation (hidden) or repeat the goal the intro shows. The chart-juice lap replaced the pause menu's copy (`board.menuButton`, `pauseTitle`, `resumeButton`, `quitButton`) with `board.homeButton` ("Naar het begin"): the board has a home button instead of a pause menu (chart-juice D5).
- **Follower comments content (follower-chat D2, feed-comments D1):** six invented followers in `FOLLOWERS` (`services/followers.ts`) (Gamer_Gijs, FatbikeFem, MemeKoning, Oma Riet, Nina_Checkt, KortingKevin), each with 3+ lines for viral, ad and news and 1–3 for the state cues; Oma Riet and Nina_Checkt are critical (complain, then leave on low reputation). The `toxic` lines are mean the way comments get mean (ratio, "huil maar"), never slurs or threats; the critics' `toxic` lines name it as bullying. Comment labels are in `UI_COPY.comments`. Same tone rules as the deck: no real people, brands or games.
- **Tone:** satirical and playful Dutch for 11-year-olds. No real people, brands, parties, games or platforms; describe them generically ("dat battle-royale-spel", "een brawler-game", "een streamer", "een fatbike"); invented names only (MoonCoin, De Theepot). News copy is deliberately calm (design § 5).
- **Kids content (kids-content D1–D7):** hard words are marked `[[woord]]` or `[[vorm|term]]` and explained in `GLOSSARY`; markup only in prose fields that render through the kit's `GlossaryText` (card body and flavor, event body, phase intro body and tip, game-over body and lesson, end report intro, how-to-play, tutorial, privacy, credits body), never in titles, labels or buttons; feed posts and announcements strip it. The deck was scored for 11-year-olds (relatable / lesson / age fit, table in the lap brief): 20 adult-world items removed, 40 added from the pupils' world (skins, loot boxes — never "verrassingskist", owner-photos D4 — streamers, memes, challenges, fatbikes, group chats). Effects of new cards mirror the removed ones per phase, so no retune was needed. Phase names are kept (design § 6); the intros explain _scale-up_ and _beursgang_. The phase-intro-copy lap rewrote the four intro bodies as second-person scenes (where you are, what just happened, the dilemma as a question; phase 1 from the owner's text, with _boost_ in the glossary); each body is at most four lines at 360 px wide, so the intro still fits one 360×640 screen with the phase photo. "Je eerste shitstorm" is "Je eerste ophef" (id kept). Dialogs with marked words in their description (events, card detail) start keyboard focus on the first control after the description (kit `Modal`), so a glossary word is never the starting point.
- **Events show scaled numbers (for the board and endings laps):** option effects are raw content values; the engine scales them per phase (negative reputation ×0.5 / ×1 / ×2 / ×2.5, positive revenue ×0.5 in phase 1). A raw −8 reputation is −20 at the IPO, so the event UI must show the phase-scaled effect, not the content value.
- **End report numbers are satire (D7):** `buildEndReport` derives each line from the run (played types, users, revenue, turns, fired events) with fixed scale factors; they read as plausible, not as statistics.
- **Game-over text matches the run (D9; ending-texts D1–D2; score-redesign D16):** Bankroet is the only game over; `GAME_OVER_TEXTS.bankrupt` has four stories and `selectGameOverText(reason, run)` picks one from the run's scandals and played card types, so the ending does not blame ads the player never showed: 2+ scandals → `scandals`; else an ad share of 50%+ → `noAudience`; else under 25% ads → `noIncome`; else `costsOutgrew`. Milestone events (investors, worldwide, roadshow) are not scandals. The thresholds are exported (`NO_INCOME_BELOW_AD_SHARE`, `AD_HEAVY_SHARE`, `SCANDALS_FROM_EVENTS`). The Verlaten and Verbod texts were folded into `noAudience` and `scandals`. The selection lives here, next to the texts and `buildEndReport`, because it decides which copy fits; game-flow only renders it. Event choices are not in the run summary, so no story claims which option the player picked.

All 28 events fire in some simulated game; no strategy has a back-to-back event. Tuning record: with the first draft values (decisions at or slightly under the average post) rule-of-thumb fell to 27–30 wins. Experiments isolated the cause: new events alone (old deck) gave 39 wins, and taking the decisions out of phase 3 alone gave 37, while neutralising their effects made it worse (21). Six decisions per type dilute the stronger phase-3 posts, so the phase-3 pool paid less. Lifting the decision values to the level of the phase posts they sit next to (viral users 13–16, ads € 17–21 with reputation −1 to −3, news users 4–5 and reputation 8–10) restored it. Human-like play gets a little easier and phase 4 a little shorter (11 → 9 turns): its half-decision pool of 8 cards per type holds no weak cards. Accepted: inside the 25–45-turn target.

## Score model tuning (score-redesign lap)

- **Phase numbers (score-redesign D11, D26):** each goal is about 3× the previous one (100k / 300k / 1M / 3M users; € 150k / € 600k / € 1.2M / € 4M), with a users gain worth 1.5k / 8k / 25k / 75k per point. A ×10 step per phase (the old 100k → 5M → 20M) put a phase's entry at only 5–7 points of users, so one news card right after a phase change wiped a third of the users. Users losses are relative (`userLossPerPoint` 2 % per point), so they never wipe the users out; gains stay per phase, so growth does not compound (relative gains made viral-only win 33 of 60).
- **Ads need an audience (D20, superseded by exponential-growth D1):** money gains pay € 0,05 per current user per point, so an ad is worth nothing at 0 users and ad-only, which loses users every play and never grows them, goes bankrupt on its server costs.
- **Toxicity drain (D21):** only Toxicity above 50 drains reputation (5 % per point): a drain on all Toxicity pinned it near 100 for every player, so the warning never went away.
- **Scandal fine (D25):** 16 revenue points per scandal; 10 or less let viral-only win some games.
- **Scandal tuning after Droogte (balance-cleanup D4):** scandal `rampPoints` 15 → 20, `maxChance` 0.7 → 0.9, `finePoints` 16 → 18. With equal type odds and no Droogte, viral-only won 5 of 60 (a few runs sprint through phases 3–4 at reputation 0 between milestones). A higher maximum only bites at the bottom of reputation, where only a news-neglecting player sits. Lost: stronger reputation decay, toxicity drain or a news-ignored +3 (each cut rule-of-thumb wins to 26–33 of 60 or rotate to 44–47 and still let viral-only win), a fine of 20 alone (rule-of-thumb 24–26 of 60), a weaker Echo-Chamber (no effect: the winners were not on streaks).
