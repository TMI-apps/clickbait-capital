# Game board

The playable board of Clickbait Capital at `/spel`: one run of the core loop over the rules engine. Rules SSOT: `documentation/DOC_GAME_DESIGN.md`; engine: `src/features/game-engine/README.md`.

## Purpose

- Shows the three meters (Inkomsten, Gebruikers, Reputatie), the phase with its milestone progress, the central phone feed and the 3-card draft (cards in the engine's drawn order, any mix of types; shuffled-hand D1).
- Lets the player open a card's details by tap or keyboard (arrow keys + Enter / Space) and feed it from there, or drag it onto the feed; answer pending events, see the end of a run, open card details and pause.
- No game rules and no run state here: the run (engine state, accepted steps, device save) is game-session's `useGameSession`; `useBoardSession` derives the board view, feed, meter deltas and sounds from it.

## Decisions

- **Content is input (game-board D1, D9):** `GameBoard` takes a `GameContent`; `src/pages/GamePage.tsx` passes `GAME_CONTENT` from game-content. A card's optional `flavor` field (game-content D2) shows in the detail view. Player copy comes from `UI_COPY`; end texts are game-flow's.
- **Scaled numbers (D10):** draft cards and event options show effects after the engine's `scaleForPhase` (e.g. reputation loss ×2 in phase 3), so the number shown is the number applied. Turn modifiers (Ad-Fatigue, Echo-Chamber) are not previewed. Event options reuse the kit's `GameCardEffects`.
- **Tap opens details (D2, replaced by thumbnail-imagery D9):** a tap or Enter / Space opens the detail dialog, whose "In de feed" button plays the card; dragging onto the feed plays in one move. No info button on the artwork (it covered the thumbnail). Focus returns to the same draft slot when the dialog closes.
- **Drag (D3, mobile-fit D1):** `@dnd-kit/core` with one `PointerSensor` (mouse, touch, pen) that starts a drag on the first move past 6px, no press-and-hold; a press without that move stays a click, so tap-to-details (D2) keeps working (dnd-kit swallows the click after a real drag). Draft cards are `touch-action: none` so the browser never takes the gesture for scrolling. Dropping on the feed plays the card. No keyboard drag sensor: the keyboard path is arrows + Enter.
- **Fullscreen (D4):** `/spel` hides the Topbar (`FULLSCREEN_ROUTES` in `src/App.tsx`); quitting goes through the pause menu.
- **Fits the screen (mobile-fit D2–D6):** the board is one `h-dvh` grid that never scrolls: portrait stacks header, meters, feed (takes the rest, scrolls its own posts) and draft; the `board-split` variant (`src/index.css`: sideways phone up to 40rem high) puts header, meters and feed left and the draft right. The feed (min 6rem) absorbs short screens; cards scale with the column width (no height cap: below ~500px tall portrait the draft bottom clips). Top / bottom padding grow into the notch and home-indicator insets (`viewport-fit=cover`; the body pads the sides). `data-viewport-lock` on the board root stops document scroll, rubber-band and pull-to-refresh; `touch-action: manipulation` stops double-tap zoom, pinch zoom stays (WCAG 1.4.4). The installed app keeps `display: standalone`.
- **Desktop board (desktop-layout D1–D6):** the `board-desk` variant (`src/index.css`: ≥ 64rem wide and more than 40rem high; never overlaps `board-split`) makes two columns: a play column with the feed and the draft right under it (so a drag is short) and a status panel (`minmax(18rem, 22rem)`) with the header (phase, goal, progress, turn, sound and pause), the meters stacked as larger tiles and the Journalistieke Droogte notice (`DroughtNotice`, hidden in `CardDraft` on desk). The play column is as wide as the phone feed (24rem), narrower on desk screens under ~720px high (`min(24rem, 100dvh - 21rem)`) so the newest post and the whole hand fit; on desk the newest post always uses the stacked photo-on-top layout, so feed posts look like the earlier tall desktop feed (D5). The status panel is not a scroll container: the meter juice marks poke out of their tiles and would otherwise make it scroll sideways (D6). `PlayingBoard` wraps status and play in two `display: contents` divs that only become flex columns on desk, so the phone grids are untouched and the DOM order (status before play) is the reading order everywhere.
- **Answer-only dialogs (D5):** events and the end screen use `Modal` with `dismissible={false}` (no close button, Escape and overlay ignored); the pause and detail dialogs pass `closeLabel="Sluiten"` (the kit default stays "Close").
- **Droogte (D6):** during Journalistieke Droogte every viral card in the draft gets a destructive ring and warning icon, and a text line above the draft explains it (colour is never the only signal).
- **Compact cards (D7, D8):** the three cards stay in one row at 390px using `GameCard size="compact"` at every width; the flavor text and full labels are in the detail dialog.
- **Meter change:** the delta chip shows the change since the previous action including passive costs, not the card's printed effect.
- **Stats only as goal progress (D11):** the absolute stats (engine D1) show only for the goal conditions that read them, as "Bereik: x / doel (Gebruikers ≥ N)" and "Omzet: x / doel". Bereik is the number behind the Gebruikers meter (engine D1), so the users line names the meter level `N` that reaches the goal in this phase (`meterForUsers`, computed from content, no number in copy) and its progress moves with the meter. Phase 4 (meter goal) shows none. The end screen reports the run's peak reach (`peakUsers`, "Hoogste bereik") instead of the last value, so a player who held 20M never reads 11M at the IPO; game-flow and the end report receive it through `toGameEnd`.
- **Milestone progress:** the weakest goal condition as a ratio (stat / target, meter / threshold), plus "x/N beurten op rij" when the goal needs a streak.
- **Sounds per action (D12, game-session-split D1):** `useBoardSession` derives the last action's sounds from the session (`soundsForLastStep`: none before the first action or for steps replayed from a save; `key` = step count) with the pure `soundsForAction` (card type or `event-choice`, then one follow-up: `ipo-win` / `game-over` / `phase-up` / `event-appear`, else at most one `meter-loss` / `meter-gain` when a meter moves ≥ 8) and `useBoardSounds` plays them. Dialog components stay sound-free; main buttons (pause, resume, restart, quit, the phase intro's continue) give `ui-tap` via `GameBoard`. The compact `MuteToggle` sits next to pause. Audio, mute and the gesture unlock live in `@/features/game-audio/api`.
- **Juice (game-juice D1–D7, event-juice-drop D1, D3):** `PlayingBoard` runs `useActionJuice` from the view (`juiceActionForPost` of the newest post — for an event post the card juice of the chosen option's kind, `FeedEntry.optionKind` — meter deltas, feed length). When the newest post is an answered event, `hold` keeps the meters and juice on the pre-choice view until the event dialog has closed, so count-up, flashes and particles play in sight (the sounds and the announcement keep their timing) and marks the played card's slot (`draftCardElement`) so particles start there. The board container shakes on viral, `JuiceLayer` throws the particles, `MeterPanel` counts values and shows `MeterJuice` accents and flashes, `PhoneFeed` uses `FEED_ENTRANCE` per type, the draft staggers its draw by `duration-fast`, and `GameBoard` wraps the end in `EndJuice`.
- **Wasted gains (game-juice D5):** `capEffects` in `boardView` caps draft and event-option gains at the meter's room to 100 (`overflow` → GameCard's "MAX" mark). `MeterView.full` marks a meter at 100: it glows and shows the kit `CapBadge` ("MAX") in its label row.
- **Game-flow screens (game-flow D1, D2):** events open the game-flow `EventModal` over the board (the board keeps the last event while it animates closed); phase intros and the endings replace the board (`PhaseTransitionScreen`, `GameEndFlow`). The board's `toGameEnd` gives the run summary (engine numbers plus board-formatted stat texts, game-flow D4); restart also resets the phase intros.

- **Screen readers (screen-reader D1–D3):** every card play or event answer produces one polite announcement (`describeTurn`): the post or chosen policy, each changed meter as "Inkomsten omhoog 8, nu 58", the goal line, then warnings (meter in danger; Journalistieke droogte only on the turn it starts). Texts queue while the event dialog is mounted (Radix hides the rest of the page from screen readers) and go out together 150 ms after focus has moved, so the focus speech doesn't cut them off. It replaces the old "Geplaatst: …" region and is empty after a phase intro, so no old turn repeats. Focus: board heading when the board opens, the card in the same draft slot after a play, that slot again after an event answer (`EventModal onCloseAutoFocus`). The event dialog reveals the outcome itself and calls `chooseOption` only on "Verder", so the announcement follows that step (event-redesign D2–D3). `toEventView` orders the options with a seeded shuffle (`seededOrder` in `boardView.ts`) keyed on the run seed and event id, so A is not always the money option and a resumed run shows the same order (event-redesign D5). Each meter tile is one `role="meter"` whose `valueText` (from `toMeters`) carries value, change and danger/max; the visible number, delta and icons are `aria-hidden`.

- **Settings inside the pause menu (game-settings D1):** "Instellingen" swaps the pause dialog's content for `SettingsPanel` with a "Terug" button instead of navigating to `/instellingen`, so play continues where it was without a trip through the resume offer.

- **Saved runs (local-save D1, D3, D5, D8):** game-session's `useGameSession` records every accepted action, writes the save after each one and replays a `savedRun` prop (a save that no longer replays starts a new run; see `src/features/game-session/README.md`). A resumed board skips the phase intro (`usePhaseIntro(phase, seenPhase)`) unless the save's last action started the phase (then the interrupted intro shows again, before that turn's milestone event), focuses the board heading and speaks `describeResume` once ("Spel hervat. Fase 2 van 4: Scale-up. Beurt 7." plus the meter values), queued like a turn text. Pause → "Stoppen" keeps the save, so the start screen offers "Verder spelen". The run itself moved to game-session (game-session-split), which ended the local-save D9 budget override.
- **Feed posts with pictures (feed-images D1–D5):** each `FeedEntry` carries its card or event `contentId`; `PhoneFeed` draws the picture with the kit's `GameCardArtwork` and `getCardImage` (same path as the draft: lazy, fixed 4:3 box, ThumbnailFrame except on news, type-icon placeholder for ids without a photo). The newest post shows photo, type, title and body: photo on top when the feed box is at least 24rem tall (size container query), beside the text otherwise, so it is always fully visible. Older posts are compact rows (64 px photo without caption / sticker / marker, type, title; the body stays as screen-reader text). While a card is dragged, a drop slot shaped like the newest post (same grid and 4:3 artwork box) opens at the top of the list and pushes the posts down (its text cell is as tall as the post's type, title and body lines); over the feed it turns solid (event-juice-drop D4). It opens and closes with a grid-row height transition on `duration-normal` (instant under reduced motion) and is keyed by the newest post, so it vanishes at once when the dropped post takes its place. The list scrolls back to the top when a post lands or a drag starts; the page never scrolls. Posts have no type-coloured side bar; the card surface and the type label carry the post (readable-hover D5).
- **Feed comments (feed-comments D1–D4, supersedes the follower-chat strip):** `useBoardSession` adds `comments`, one follower-comments batch per session step (`commentsForSession`); each `FeedEntry` carries its `step`, so `PhoneFeed` puts the right batch under every post. The newest post shows all its comments (2–4), revealed one by one by `useCommentReveal` once `juice.key` (passed as `revealKey`) reaches the batch, so after an event they wait for the dialog like the meters; older posts collapse to the first comment plus "+N reacties". The feed takes the whole feed row again (no strip). The board's own announcer and the comments' `CommentAnnouncer` are separate polite regions; the comments speak once per batch, after the turn summary. A resumed run shows every post's comments at once, silently.
- **Glossary words (kids-content D3–D4):** `CardDetailDialog` renders the card body and flavor through the kit's `GlossaryText` with `GLOSSARY` (tap a marked word for its meaning; the card there is static, so no button nests in a button). Draft cards show titles only, which carry no markup. `feedEntryForCard` / `feedEntryForEvent` strip the markup, so feed posts and turn announcements are plain text.
