# Game board

The playable board of Clickbait Capital at `/spel`: one run of the core loop over the rules engine. Rules SSOT: `documentation/DOC_GAME_DESIGN.md`; engine: `src/features/game-engine/README.md`.

## Purpose

- Shows the three meters (Inkomsten, Gebruikers, Reputatie), the phase with its milestone progress, the central phone feed and the 3-card draft (cards in the engine's drawn order, mostly one of each type, no card twice in a row; shuffled-hand D1, deck-draw D1–D4).
- Lets the player open a card's details by tap or keyboard (arrow keys + Enter / Space) and feed it from there, or drag it onto the feed; answer pending events, see the end of a run, open card details and pause.
- No game rules and no run state here: the run (engine state, accepted steps, device save) is game-session's `useGameSession`; `useBoardSession` derives the board view, feed, score tiles and sounds from it.

## Decisions

- **Content is input (game-board D1, D9):** `GameBoard` takes a `GameContent`; `src/pages/GamePage.tsx` passes `GAME_CONTENT` from game-content. A card's optional `flavor` field (game-content D2) shows in the detail view. Player copy comes from `UI_COPY`; end texts are game-flow's.
- **Numbers shown are numbers applied (D10, score-redesign D15):** draft cards and event options show the engine's own result of the play or choice (see Card effects), turn modifiers included. Event options reuse the kit's `GameCardEffects`.
- **Tap opens details (D2, replaced by thumbnail-imagery D9):** a tap or Enter / Space opens the detail dialog, whose "In de feed" button plays the card; dragging onto the feed plays in one move. No info button on the artwork (it covered the thumbnail). Focus returns to the same draft slot when the dialog closes.
- **Drag (D3, mobile-fit D1):** `@dnd-kit/core` with one `PointerSensor` (mouse, touch, pen) that starts a drag on the first move past 6px, no press-and-hold; a press without that move stays a click, so tap-to-details (D2) keeps working (dnd-kit swallows the click after a real drag). Draft cards are `touch-action: none` so the browser never takes the gesture for scrolling. Dropping on the feed plays the card. No keyboard drag sensor: the keyboard path is arrows + Enter.
- **Fullscreen (D4):** `/spel` hides the Topbar (`FULLSCREEN_ROUTES` in `src/App.tsx`); quitting goes through the pause menu.
- **Fits the screen (mobile-fit D2–D6):** the board is one `h-dvh` grid that never scrolls: portrait stacks header, meters, feed (takes the rest, scrolls its own posts) and draft; the `board-split` variant (`src/index.css`: sideways phone up to 40rem high) puts header, meters and feed left and the draft right. The feed (min 6rem) absorbs short screens; cards scale with the column width (no height cap: below ~500px tall portrait the draft bottom clips). Top / bottom padding grow into the notch and home-indicator insets (`viewport-fit=cover`; the body pads the sides). `data-viewport-lock` on the board root stops document scroll, rubber-band and pull-to-refresh; `touch-action: manipulation` stops double-tap zoom, pinch zoom stays (WCAG 1.4.4). The installed app keeps `display: standalone`.
- **Desktop board (desk-wide D1, replaces desktop-layout D1 / D4's placement; D3, D5 hold):** the `board-desk` variant (`src/index.css`: ≥ 64rem wide and more than 40rem high; never overlaps `board-split`) fills the whole viewport (no centred max width) with two columns: a status column on the left that takes all the remaining width and height (header with phase, turn, sound and pause; the score chart, which grows to fill the column; the scandal warning under it) and a play column pinned to the right edge, `clamp(22rem, 30vw, 28rem)` wide, with the feed and the hand right under it (a short drag). At 1024 px the chart is ~600 px wide, at 2560 px ~2000 px. `PlayingBoard` wraps status and play in two `display: contents` divs that only become flex columns on desk, so the phone grids are untouched and the DOM order (status before play) is the reading order everywhere, now also the visual order. On desk the `MeterPanel` section and chart are `flex-1` instead of a fixed height, and the left counter hugs its content so its juice glow stays its own size. The status column is not a scroll container: the meter juice marks poke out of their counters and would otherwise make it scroll (desktop-layout D5).
- **Answer-only dialogs (D5):** events and the end screen use `Modal` with `dismissible={false}` (no close button, Escape and overlay ignored); the pause and detail dialogs pass `closeLabel="Sluiten"` (the kit default stays "Close").
- **No modifier notice (balance-cleanup D1, replaces D6 and desktop-layout D2):** the Journalistieke Droogte ring, icon and text line are gone with the engine rule; draft cards carry no modifier flag.
- **Compact cards (D7, D8):** the three cards stay in one row at 390px using `GameCard size="compact"` at every width; the flavor text and full labels are in the detail dialog.
- **Score chart (score-redesign D10, D23, D29):** `MeterPanel` shows `ScoreChart` from `@/features/score-chart/api` (both numbers on one chart, each on its own scale so its dashed goal line sits at its own height in the number's colour, labelled with its value, D31; history from `toScoreHistory`: the whole run, the start values plus the state after every action, each with its phase and answered event, as shares of the current goal on a zero-based axis; `toScoreMarkers` gives the phase dividers ("F2") and the scandal / world-event ticks; the run is rebuilt from the session steps, so a resumed run keeps it; a held card's `forecastText` labels its forecast dot, chart-timeline D1–D7) and two counters, Gebruikers and Geld, in the chart's top corners on a translucent backing, passed as `ScoreChart` children, which the chart lays over its top and measures so the lines, goal labels and forecast stay below them (chart-timeline D6; the name row shows on the desk layout only; the panel has no padding, the counters carry the inset, so the lines start in the panel's bottom-left corner, chart-origin D2) (amounts never wrap; € 4.000.000 fits at every width) (tile keys `users` / `revenue`, so the juice keys stay the meter keys): the absolute amount (a debt reads "−€ 24.000", like the change chips, also on the results and share card), the last action's absolute change (`TurnReport.delta`, incl. passive costs and scandal fines); "Doel: …" is spoken only (`valueText`), the chart shows it. A reached line glows with a check; Geld at or below 3 turns of costs is in danger (red, "bijna bankroet"). The Reputatie tile is gone: reputation is hidden (score-redesign D13). `ScandalWarning` under the tiles shows "Schandaal dreigt: N% kans per beurt" (`toScandalRisk`, the engine's `scandalChance`) only while reputation is below the threshold; tiles and warning are one grid item, so the phone grid keeps its rows.
- **Goal lines (D11, score-redesign D17, D30, D31):** the goal is shown only in the chart, as one dashed goal line per number in that number's colour with its value; the header keeps phase name and turn, plus the goal sentence for screen readers (sr-only). The turn announcement still reads "Gebruikers: x / doel" and "Geld: x / doel" (`goalStats`). The end screen reports the run's peak users (`peakUsers`, "Hoogste bereik") and the final money, through `toGameEnd`.
- **Juice per action (D12, juice-migrate D1–D4, D9; replaces the sound ids and `useActionJuice`):** the board emits game-juice catalog events and never calls effects or sounds. Handlers emit the action itself: a play emits `card.played` (variant = card type, origin = the card, still in the draft; a card no longer in the draft plays nothing), an answer emits `event.choice` (and `stopJuice(EVENT_OUTCOME_TAG)`, so the outcome's marks leave with the dialog), and every button taps through game-juice's app-wide `ui.press` (juice-wiring-flow D1; `withTap` is gone; the detail dialog's "In de feed" is `data-juice-press="off"`, since the play has its own juice). `useBoardSession` derives the rest from the session (`juiceForLastStep`; nothing before the first action or for steps replayed from a save; `key` = step count). The **follow-up** is `run.won`, `run.lost`, `phase.up` or `event.appeared`; `GameBoard` emits it at once (`useBoardFollowUp`), and its sound waits 250 ms in the recipe. The **reveal** is `event.resolved` (option kind), one `meter.changed` per moved tile and, for a card, `feed.landed`; an event post's `feed.landed` plays at once with the follow-up, since the post appears behind the closing dialog. Tile variants: `dull` for news and journalism; otherwise gain / loss, and one tile gets `-big` (sound blip) when it moved ≥ 10 % (`METER_CUE_THRESHOLD`; a loss wins), unless a milestone followed. Sounds, timing and looks live in the recipes (`src/features/game-juice/services/gameJuiceRecipes.ts`); the compact `MuteToggle` sits next to pause.
- **Juice anchors and reveal (event-juice-drop D1, D3, juice-migrate D5, D6):** `PlayingBoard` runs `useBoardReveal` (key = feed length). When the newest post is an answered event, `hold` keeps the meters on the pre-choice view and holds the reveal until the event dialog has closed, so flashes and particles play in sight. The held key also restarts the delta chips and reveals the newest comments. Anchors: the board grid is `board`, the score chart panel is `chart`, each counter is `meter:users` / `meter:revenue`, the scandal warning is `scandal`, the newest post is `feed:newest`, and `GameBoard` wraps the end flow in `end-screen`. The drop slot is `feed:drop` and the newest shown comment `comment:latest` (via `PostComments`' `latestRef`). The draft still staggers its draw in CSS (kit `card-draw`).
- **Moment-to-moment juice (juice-wiring-board D1–D7):** `useCardForecast` also emits the drag's moments: `card.pickup` on a drag start or a hold (origin = the card via `draftCardElement`, passed in; a hold that turns into a drag lifts once), `card.over-feed` from dnd-kit's `onDragOver`, `card.cancel` when a drag ends off the feed (all three silent: juice-tone-down D1–D2) (`endDrag` returns whether it was a play), and `forecast.shown` when the previewed card changes. `useBoardReveal` adds `goal.reached` / `money.danger` (`meterMoments`) on the edge between the tiles shown before and now. `useScandalWarningJuice` ramps `scandal.warning` per level and stops it by tag when the warning goes. `useCommentArrivals` emits `comment.arrived` per newly shown comment. `juiceForLastStep` swaps `event.appeared` for `scandal.hit` when the opened event is a scandal and passes each tile's `deltaText` as the floater text. All of it stays quiet at mount (a resume), and nothing of the last action reveals on a run's end: `GameBoard` renders the end flow instead of `PlayingBoard`, so goal / danger moments never stack on `run.won` / `run.lost`.
- **Card effects (score-redesign D15, D23):** draft cards show only Geld and Gebruikers rows, as the absolute change the play makes (`toDraft` plays the card on a copy of the state with the pure engine `playCard`: phase scaling, compounding growth, money per user, relative user losses and passive costs included; `amountText` like "€ 2.000", from a million on "€ 1,2 mln" (exponential-growth D4)); reputation and Toxicity stay hidden. Event options do the same with the engine's `resolveEvent` on a copy (scandal options include the fine). There is no meter cap any more, so `capEffects` and the "MAX" marks are gone.
- **Forecast (score-redesign D12, D32):** every `DraftCardView` carries `forecast` (where each line lands after the play, as a share of its goal line, from the same `playCard` copy). `useCardForecast` picks the previewed card (dragged, else held, else open in the detail dialog, else keyboard-focused via `:focus-visible`) and `MeterPanel` passes its forecast to the chart, which draws a dashed segment to a hollow dot. Holding uses `useLongPress` from `src/shared/hooks/` (350 ms, any pointer) next to the dnd-kit listeners: a short press stays a tap (details), a move past 6px stays a drag, and the click after a hold is swallowed. Draft cards are `select-none` without the iOS callout; the context menu is blocked on them. The draft's arrow keys use `useRovingFocus` from `src/shared/hooks/` (both generic input hooks moved out to keep the feature within its file budget; `useRetainedValue`, which keeps a dialog's content while it animates closed, followed in juice-migrate). On phones the counters drop their name row (icon + amount + change) so the chart keeps its height.
- **Game-flow screens (game-flow D1, D2):** events open the game-flow `EventModal` over the board (the board keeps the last event while it animates closed); phase intros and the endings replace the board (`PhaseTransitionScreen`, `GameEndFlow`). The board's `toGameEnd` gives the run summary (engine numbers plus board-formatted stat texts, game-flow D4); restart also resets the phase intros.

- **Screen readers (screen-reader D1–D3):** every card play or event answer produces one polite announcement (`describeTurn`): the post or chosen policy, each changed number as "Geld +€ 14.000, nu € 114.000", the goal lines, then warnings (money nearly gone, the scandal risk on every turn it shows; the Droogte line went with the rule, balance-cleanup D1). Texts queue while the event dialog is mounted (Radix hides the rest of the page from screen readers) and go out together 150 ms after focus has moved, so the focus speech doesn't cut them off. It replaces the old "Geplaatst: …" region and is empty after a phase intro, so no old turn repeats. Focus: board heading when the board opens, the card in the same draft slot after a play, that slot again after an event answer (`EventModal onCloseAutoFocus`). The event dialog reveals the outcome itself and calls `chooseOption` only on "Verder", so the announcement follows that step (event-redesign D2–D3). `toEventView` orders the options with a seeded shuffle (`seededOrder` in `boardView.ts`) keyed on the run seed and event id, so A is not always the money option and a resumed run shows the same order (event-redesign D5). Each meter tile is one `role="meter"` whose `valueText` (from `toMeters`) carries amount, change, goal line and danger; the visible number, delta and icons are `aria-hidden`.

- **Settings inside the pause menu (game-settings D1):** "Instellingen" swaps the pause dialog's content for `SettingsPanel` with a "Terug" button instead of navigating to `/instellingen`, so play continues where it was without a trip through the resume offer.

- **Saved runs (local-save D1, D3, D5, D8):** game-session's `useGameSession` records every accepted action, writes the save after each one and replays a `savedRun` prop (a save that no longer replays starts a new run; see `src/features/game-session/README.md`). A resumed board skips the phase intro (`usePhaseIntro(phase, seenPhase)`) unless the save's last action started the phase (then the interrupted intro shows again, before that turn's milestone event), focuses the board heading and speaks `describeResume` once ("Spel hervat. Fase 2 van 4: Scale-up. Beurt 7." plus the meter values), queued like a turn text. Pause → "Stoppen" keeps the save, so the start screen offers "Verder spelen". The run itself moved to game-session (game-session-split), which ended the local-save D9 budget override.
- **Feed posts with pictures (feed-images D1–D5):** each `FeedEntry` carries its card or event `contentId`; `PhoneFeed` draws the picture with the kit's `GameCardArtwork` and `getCardImage` (same path as the draft: lazy, fixed 4:3 box, ThumbnailFrame except on news, type-icon placeholder for ids without a photo). The newest post is a card with the photo full-width on top, then type, title and body, on every screen size. The photo is a banner cropped around its focal point: `max(4rem, min(12rem, 100cqh − 7rem, 4:3 height))`, so it is at most 12rem tall and on a short box (phones around 390×664, landscape) shrinks so type, title and body stay visible under it (the feed list is a size container) (feed-top-card D1–D3 replaces feed-images D3, which put the photo beside the text below a 24rem feed box and read as a compact row on most phones). `GameBoard.test.tsx` locks this structure. Older posts are compact rows (64 px photo without caption / sticker / marker, type, title; the body stays as screen-reader text). While a card is dragged, a drop slot shaped like the newest post (same photo height on top) opens at the top of the list and pushes the posts down (its text cell is as tall as the post's type, title and body lines); over the feed it turns solid (event-juice-drop D4). It opens and closes with a grid-row height transition on `duration-normal` (instant under reduced motion) and is keyed by the newest post, so it vanishes at once when the dropped post takes its place. The list scrolls back to the top when a post lands or a drag starts; the page never scrolls. Posts have no type-coloured side bar; the card surface and the type label carry the post (readable-hover D5).
- **Feed comments (feed-comments D1–D4, supersedes the follower-chat strip):** `useBoardSession` adds `comments`, one follower-comments batch per session step (`commentsForSession`); each `FeedEntry` carries its `step`, so `PhoneFeed` puts the right batch under every post. The newest post shows all its comments (2–4), revealed one by one by `useCommentReveal` once `juice.key` (passed as `revealKey`) reaches the batch, so after an event they wait for the dialog like the meters; older posts collapse to the first comment plus "+N reacties". The feed takes the whole feed row again (no strip). The board's own announcer and the comments' `CommentAnnouncer` are separate polite regions; the comments speak once per batch, after the turn summary. A resumed run shows every post's comments at once, silently.
- **Glossary words (kids-content D3–D4):** `CardDetailDialog` renders the card body and flavor through the kit's `GlossaryText` with `GLOSSARY` (tap a marked word for its meaning; the card there is static, so no button nests in a button). Draft cards show titles only, which carry no markup. `feedEntryForCard` / `feedEntryForEvent` strip the markup, so feed posts and turn announcements are plain text.
